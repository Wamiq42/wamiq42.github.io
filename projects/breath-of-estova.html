Overview

Breath of Estova is a 2D pixel-art MMORPG in development at Magami Studios — a cozy online world set on Kohira's Isle, with towns, dungeons, and boss encounters to explore with other players. As the game is still unreleased, I can only share what the studio has made public.

My contribution

  • Architectural upgrades. Drove key architecture changes to improve performance, maintainability, and production scalability across the codebase.
  • Long-term live systems. Designed and built robust systems intended to support continuous product growth and stability — the foundation an MMORPG needs to keep shipping content.
  • Core gameplay & features. Implemented gameplay elements, mechanics, and major consumer-facing features.
