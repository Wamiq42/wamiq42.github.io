Overview

Breath of Estova is a 2D pixel-art MMORPG by Magami Studios — a cozy online world set on Kohira's Isle, with towns, dungeons, and boss encounters to explore with other players. The game was unreleased while I worked on it, so I can only share what the studio has made public.
