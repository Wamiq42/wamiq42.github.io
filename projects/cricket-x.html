Overview

Cricket X is a VR cricket simulator with realistic physics, built to sharpen batting skills with fully customizable deliveries — swing, spin, speed, bowler type, bounce, and pitch pace. It ships with Challenge, Training, and Demo modes on Meta Quest.

The project was large enough that development was split between two developers to speed up system building and fixes. I co-developed several of the game's major systems.

02 / 04

My contribution

  • Fielding system. One of the game's major gameplay systems — fielder behavior and the logic that turns a batted ball into a live cricket play.
  • Physics system optimization. Performance and accuracy work on the ball physics to keep the simulation believable at Quest frame rates.
  • Animation systems. Animation-related work across the game's characters.
  • Feature development & support. Rapid feature implementation and gameplay enhancements alongside the core team whenever the workload demanded it.

03 / 04

Back on Cricket X in 2026

Since returning to XRev in June 2026:

  • Two-headset local multiplayer (in testing). Two players bat together on separate headsets over local Wi-Fi: each headset streams its player's head, bat and pads 90 times a second, and the partner's headset mirrors the striker's bowler, fielders and ball.
  • A live TV spectator view. A screen beside the player replays each ball from the headset's event stream — bowler, flight, bounce, shot and fielders. I cut its delay behind the headset from about 0.3 s to about 30 ms, and it no longer freezes when the Wi-Fi hiccups.
  • Fielding realism. Fielders walk in with the bowler and take the ball the way it comes to them, and catches can now be dropped — held 60% of the time on Easy, 75% on Medium and 85% on Hard.
  • Companion and accessibility. Companion controls for venues that work over local Wi-Fi without internet, and a Bat Shield setting that widens the bat to make the ball easier to hit.
